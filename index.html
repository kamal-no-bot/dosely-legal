Dosely Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Dosely is a medication reminder built on real alarms. The list of medicines a person takes is among the most sensitive data a phone can hold, and Dosely is built around that fact. The short version:

Your medication data stays on your device and in your own private iCloud. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no tracking and no data sale, ever. We cannot read your medications, your schedule or your history.

Your medications and dose history

Medications, schedules, dose records, notes and refill counts are stored in a database on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, that database syncs through your own private iCloud (CloudKit) database, under your Apple Account. We operate no server, we hold no copy and we cannot read it. Sync can be turned off in Settings at any time.

Apple Health (read-only)

With your permission, Dosely reads two things from Apple Health: the medication list you already keep there, so you do not have to type it twice, and medication dose events, so a dose you log in Health confirms the matching dose here instead of asking you twice.

Dosely writes nothing to Health. Authorization is requested per medication, and only when you open the import screen, never at launch. Health data is never included in anything that leaves the device. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing, or in Settings, Privacy and Security, Health.

Alarms and notifications

Alarms and reminders are scheduled by your device, from your own schedule. Nothing is sent through a server, and no push notification service is used. Turning on Discreet mode keeps medication names out of every alarm, notification, Live Activity and widget, so a name never appears on your Lock Screen.

App lock

You can optionally require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open Dosely, and the app is covered in the app switcher so a medication list cannot be read from a snapshot. Authentication is performed by the system; Dosely never receives your biometric data. Answering an alarm is deliberately never gated behind the lock.

Exports you create

CSV exports, the adherence report and the caregiver summary are generated on your device and go only where you send them. Dosely does not upload them anywhere.

Purchases

Dosely Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account.

Deleting your data

Deleting a medication removes it and its records from the app. Deleting Dosely removes the on-device database entirely. If you used iCloud sync, the copy in your private iCloud database is removed when you delete the app's data in Settings, Apple Account, iCloud, Manage Account Storage.

Dosely collects nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter that receives your content, and no advertising identifier in any target, including the widgets and the Apple Watch app.

Children's privacy

Dosely is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

What Dosely is not

Dosely is a reminder and record-keeping tool, not a medical device. It gives no medical advice, no dosing guidance and no drug interaction warnings. Schedules, taper phases and as-needed intervals are values you enter from your own prescriber.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes.

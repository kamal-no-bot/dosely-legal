Common questions

My alarm did not ring

Check three things, in this order. First, Settings, Dosely, and confirm alarm permission is granted; Dosely shows a banner in the app when it is not. Second, confirm the medication is set to alarm grade: on the free tier one medication gets the full alarm treatment and the rest send standard reminders. Third, confirm the dose time has not already been logged, since a logged dose does not ring.

What is the difference between an alarm and a reminder?

An alarm is full screen and sounds even when your phone is on Silent or in a Focus mode, the same way a Clock app alarm behaves. A standard reminder is an ordinary notification and obeys Silent and Focus. Alarms are the reason Dosely exists.

Stopping the alarm did not mark the dose as taken

That is deliberate. Stopping only silences the sound. A dose stays unresolved until you log it as taken or skipped, which is what makes the history worth anything. You can log from the alarm, from the notification without unlocking, from the widget, from your Apple Watch, from Control Center, by asking Siri, or in the app.

What is free, and what is Pro?

Free forever: unlimited medications with standard reminders, every schedule type, one medication with the full alarm treatment, your complete history and stats, the Apple Watch app, the next dose widget, discreet mode, Siri and Shortcuts, and a CSV export of everything.

Dosely Pro adds alarms for every medication rather than one, escalation rules you control, refill tracking counted from your own schedule, a printable adherence report for your doctor, and the heatmap widgets.

I paid but Pro is locked

Open the purchase screen and tap Restore Purchases, making sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Subscriptions are Family Shareable, so a family organizer's purchase covers the family.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the next renewal and Pro stays active until the period ends.

My widget or complication shows nothing

Open Dosely once after installing or updating so it can write a fresh snapshot, then check that at least one dose is scheduled ahead. A Pro tile on the free tier reads "Open Dosely to enable" by design.

Importing from Apple Health

Settings tab, Apple Health section at the top, Import medications from Health. It is free and never behind the paywall. Dosely reads only, and writes nothing back to Health. If nothing appears, confirm you have medications saved in the Health app itself and that you allowed access when asked.

Does my data sync between my devices?

Yes, optionally, through your own private iCloud. Turn it on in Settings. Nothing passes through a server of ours.

How do I get my data out?

Settings, then export. CSV is free and contains everything. Pro adds a printable PDF adherence report and a shorter caregiver summary.

Which devices does Dosely need?

Dosely requires iOS 26.0 or later on iPhone or iPad. This is not a casual minimum: the real alarm feature is built on Apple's AlarmKit, which shipped with iOS 26 and cannot be back-deployed. The Apple Watch app requires watchOS 26.0.

A note on what Dosely is

Dosely reminds and records. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice, no dosing guidance and no drug interaction warnings. Schedules, taper phases and as-needed intervals are values you enter from your own prescriber. Always follow your prescriber and your pharmacist over anything an app tells you.
